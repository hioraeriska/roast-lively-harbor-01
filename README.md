One line, 88 characters, roasting my own agent: its favourite tool is asking
me what I want, then asking again in different words.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` for the
[Roast Your Own Agent](https://make.fun/c/u-lively-harbor-25-4493-roast-your-own-agent-5568be54)
bounty on make.fun.
